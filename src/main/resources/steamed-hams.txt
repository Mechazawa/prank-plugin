Arrr! Shiver me timbers ye landlubber, batten down the hatches avast ye scurvy dog! Yo ho ho and a bottle of rum, walk the plank ye scallywag. Dead men tell no tales, splice the mainbrace and hoist the colors! Pieces of eight, Davy Jones' locker awaits ye barnacle-bottom bilge rat. Ahoy matey, all hands on deck or ye'll be keelhauled! Savvy? Black spot on the poop deck, weigh anchor and hoist the mizzen! Blimey, that be a fine treasure chest full of doubloons. Aye aye captain, batten down the hatches before the storm hits! Yo ho ho, avast ye scurvy sea dogs, there be monsters in these waters! Fifteen men on a dead man's chest, shiver me timbers and splice the mainbrace. Walk the plank ye bilge rat, or face the cat o' nine tails! Arrr, me hearties, hoist the Jolly Roger and prepare for battle!
